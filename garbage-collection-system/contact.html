How do I report garbage?

Simply go to the Report page, fill in the details, and click submit. You can also use the "Get My Location" button to auto-fill coordinates.

How are EcoPoints calculated?

10 points per report, +5 for photos, +20 when your report is resolved, and +50 for referring friends.

Is the map updated in real-time?

Yes, new reports appear on the map within minutes of submission.

How do I contact support?
